# Red Ventures

> Red Ventures is a privately held digital marketing and media holding company headquartered in Fort Mill, South Carolina. It operates a portfolio of consumer comparison, commerce, education and content brands — Bankrate, The Points Guy, Lonely Planet, Allconnect, Sage Home Loans, RV Education and RV Home Client Services — combining proprietary technology, data science and performance marketing to acquire customers on behalf of those brands and their partners.

## Important notes for agents

- Red Ventures publishes **no public developer API program**. There is no developer portal, no API reference, no OpenAPI/AsyncAPI/GraphQL contract, and no first-party SDK on any Red Ventures-controlled host as of 2026-08-13.
- `api.redventures.com`, `developer.redventures.com`, `developers.redventures.com`, `docs.redventures.com` and `status.redventures.com` do not resolve.
- Every `/.well-known/` discovery path (`security.txt`, `openid-configuration`, `oauth-authorization-server`, `api-catalog`, `ai-plugin.json`, `agent-card.json`, `agent.json`) returns HTTP 404 on both `redventures.com` and `www.redventures.com`.
- `www.redventures.com/robots.txt` sets `Disallow: /` for the major AI crawler user agents (ClaudeBot, GPTBot, anthropic-ai, Google-Extended, CCBot, Bytespider, Amazonbot, PerplexityBot-adjacent agents and others). Respect it.
- Product APIs, where they exist, belong to the individual portfolio brands on their own domains — not to the Red Ventures corporate entity. Do not attribute a brand's API to Red Ventures without checking that brand's own documentation.
